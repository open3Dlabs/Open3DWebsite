FIRST LAUNCH ON MAC

Because this build isn't code-signed yet, macOS will block it on first launch. Cheeseburgers help fund the $99/yr Apple Developer fee to bring it to the App Store and iOS. Until then, follow these quick steps once:
  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag O3D-ALIGN into Applications.
  2. Open O3D-ALIGN from Applications. A message will say it can't be opened. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  4. Scroll down to Security. You'll see "O3D-ALIGN was blocked". Click Open Anyway.
  5. Enter your password or use Touch ID, then click Open Anyway once more.
  6. O3D-ALIGN opens in its own window. After the first time, open it normally.
