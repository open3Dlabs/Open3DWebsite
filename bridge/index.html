O3D-BRIDGE

A retro sci-fi command console for your Mac. Matrix-green on black, always on in your menu bar, with everything you glance at in one screen.

Built mostly for people who live on the Mexico and US border, with live border waits, the Tijuana street dollar and beach water quality for Tijuana and San Diego. More borders are coming soon.

FIRST LAUNCH

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag O3D-BRIDGE into Applications.
  2. The app is not notarized by Apple yet, so the first time, right-click it and choose Open, then Open again. After that it opens normally.
  3. Look for it in your menu bar. Click the icon to show the window.
